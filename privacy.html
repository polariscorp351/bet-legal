Privacy Policy for BET

Last Updated: [September 5, 2026]

1. Information We Collect

BET offers a free account (via Sign in with Apple or Google) to save your tracked bets, preferences, and prediction history, and also supports limited guest browsing without an account. When you create an account, we store your chosen display name, sport and notification preferences, tracked bets and prediction history, and subscription tier in our secure cloud database.

2. Public Leaderboard

If you keep the leaderboard visible (on by default, can be turned off in Account & Settings), your display handle and accuracy statistics are visible to other users. Your real name is never shown or used as your handle.

3. AI Analyst

Messages you send to BET's AI Analyst are processed by a third-party AI provider to generate a response. Message content is used only to answer your question, not for unrelated purposes.

4. Third-Party Payment Processing

We use official Apple and Google In-App Purchase systems to process subscription payments. Apple and Google manage this payment data under their own privacy policies; BET does not have access to your credit card or banking details.

5. Data You Control

You can edit your display name, change your sport/notification preferences, and turn off your public leaderboard visibility at any time in Account & Settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change, this policy will be updated alongside that release to keep this description accurate.
